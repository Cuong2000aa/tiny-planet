# Game Design Document (GDD) — Tiny Planet

## 1. High Concept
**Tiny Planet** is a bite-sized, charming 3D adventure platformer tailored for modern web browsers. The game evokes the tactile joy and vibrant wonder of *Astro Bot*, featuring a lovable little robot navigating floating fantasy islands in a sky-high planetary archipelago.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Juicy, Tactile Movement:** B.O.T has weight, squash and stretch on jumps, lean while turning, and responsive acceleration.
2. **Clear & Delightful Feedback:** Every interaction—collecting energy or flipping a switch—is met with particle bursts, bright audio tones, and animations.
3. **Approachable Exploration:** Simple, readable level design that rewards curiosity without frustrating platforming difficulty.
4. **Instant Web Access:** Loads quickly in any modern browser without native installation.

## 3. Character: B.O.T
- **Physical Traits:**
  - Petite, rounded capsule/egg silhouette in porcelain white.
  - Cyan emissive LED screen visor with expressive animated eyes.
  - Cute bouncy antennae that jiggles during movement.
  - Little metallic feet that tap with sound.
- **Personality:** Curious, brave, cheerful, slightly clumsy.
- **Actions:**
  - Walk / Run (Smooth analog blending).
  - Jump with air control.
  - Interact (Wave / press mechanism).
  - Victory Dance upon level completion.

## 4. The Level: The Awakening Island
A 6-stage floating island sanctuary:
1. **Area 1 — Awakening Zone:** B.O.T starts on a central floral platform overlooking the scenic floating void.
2. **Area 2 — Jump Training Platforms:** Ascending stone steps floating gently, introducing jumping mechanics.
3. **Area 3 — Energy Sanctuary:** 3 glowing Cyan/Gold Energy Fragments hover over scenic ruins, with instant HUD tracker.
4. **Area 4 — Ancient Mechanism:** A glowing pedestal switch. Stepping close displays an interaction prompt.
5. **Area 5 — Energy Bridge:** Activating the switch sends a power beam that deploys glowing light-bridge planks to the exit.
6. **Area 6 — Dimensional Portal:** The ancient gateway powers on with swirling particle rings, triggering victory upon entry.

## 5. Audiovisual Style
- **Visuals:** Pastel skybox, rich grassy greens, warm terracotta stone, emissive cyan tech accents.
- **Audio:** Warm synthesizer arpeggios, crisp chime sounds for collectibles, robotic servo cues, and celebratory jingles.
